NeoStock

Inventory, sales and stock management for Algerian businesses.

NeoStock is a desktop application for shops and wholesalers in Algeria. It runs on the business's own computer and keeps its data in a database file on that machine.

What it does

  • Invoices, proformas, delivery notes, quotes and credit notes, with Algerian tax rules (TVA, timbre, NIF/NIS/RC/AI).
  • Stock, purchases, suppliers, customers and their balances.
  • A counter mode with barcode scanning, and label and ticket printing.
  • Reports: sales, margins, stock movements and the tax declaration.
  • French, Arabic and English.

Backups

NeoStock can copy the business's database to a folder on its own computer, or — if the owner chooses to connect a Google account — to that owner's own Google Drive. Backups belong to the business. They are not sent to us.
