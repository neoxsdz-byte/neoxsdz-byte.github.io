Privacy policy

NeoStock — NeoXS Dev · last updated 21 September 2026

NeoStock is inventory and sales software for Algerian businesses, published by NeoXS Dev. It runs on the user's own computer. NeoXS Dev does not collect, transmit or store personal data on our servers.

Google account and Google Drive

Connecting a Google account is optional. If a user connects one, NeoStock uses Google Drive for a single purpose: saving backup copies of that user's own database to that user's own Google Drive.

NeoStock requests the drive.file permission, which gives access only to files NeoStock itself creates. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the user's Drive.

Backup files are written to the user's Drive and are readable only by that user. We never receive them. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Limited use

NeoStock's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What is stored, and where

A business's data — its articles, customers, suppliers, invoices and stock — stays in a database file on its own computer, and in whatever backup folder the owner chooses. The Google account's sign-in token is kept on that computer, encrypted so that it cannot be used on another machine.

Withdrawing access

A user can disconnect their Google account at any time in NeoStock (Settings → System → Automatic backup), or revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in their Drive are theirs, and are deleted by deleting the files.

Children

NeoStock is business software and is not directed at children.
